How it works

Three steps to a lighter suitcase

Build a capsule once and every combination it makes becomes an outfit.

01

Add your wardrobe

Take a photo — the background is removed on your phone — or pick several pieces at once from the basics library.

02

Fill your capsule

Place pieces in a 3×3, 3×4 or 4×4 grid in your colour palette, or let Smart fill do it to suit the forecast.

03

Wear it every day

Capsify builds every outfit the grid can make and plans one look for each day of the trip.
