Privacy policy

Last updated: 11 October 2026

Capsify ("the app") is published by Capsify ("we"). Contact: capsifyapp@gmail.com.

Summary

Capsify has no account, no advertising and no analytics. Your wardrobe, photos, capsules, outfits and colour profile stay on your device. We do not run a server and we never receive your data.

Data stored on your device

  • Wardrobe items and the photos you add, capsules, packing lists, outfit history, your name and colour-season profile, and app settings.
  • Photos are chosen by you through the system camera or photo picker. The app does not request access to your whole photo library.
  • Background removal and colour detection run on your device with Google ML Kit. Photos are not uploaded.
  • The selfie used by the colour-season quiz is analysed on your device and is not saved.
  • Home-screen widgets show this same data; the pictures they need are made and kept on your device.

This data is deleted when you uninstall the app or clear its storage.

Backups

If Android backup is enabled on your phone, Android may include the app's data in your personal Google backup (Google Drive) and restore it on a new device. This backup is run by Google under your Google account and its settings; we have no access to it. You can turn it off in Android Settings › Google › Backup.

Data sent to third parties

  • Weather forecasts — MET Norway (api.met.no). When a trip has a destination, the app sends that destination's coordinates (the city you typed, rounded, not your device location) to the Norwegian Meteorological Institute to get the forecast. The request also carries the app's name and version. No identifier about you is sent.
  • Google Play services / ML Kit. The first time background removal is used, Google Play services downloads the ML Kit model. Google ML Kit may collect device and diagnostic information.
  • Google Play Billing. Premium purchases and subscriptions are processed entirely by Google Play. We do not receive your payment details.
  • Sharing. When you share an outfit card, the image is handed to the app you choose in Android's share sheet.

The app does not use your location, contacts, microphone or any advertising identifier.

Children

Capsify is not directed at children under 13.

Your choices

You can delete individual items or capsules in the app, or all data by uninstalling the app. To cancel a subscription, use Google Play › Payments & subscriptions.

Changes

We will update this page if the app's data handling changes, and change the "Last updated" date above.
